A quick start

  1. Pick a supported sign.

    Browse the gesture library or choose a practice word.

  2. Sign in view.

    Keep your hands visible. Lower them out of view to finish a gesture.

  3. Read the result.

    Predictions can change as you move. Confidence is a model score, not a guarantee.

This is a limited-vocabulary gesture recognizer, not a full sign-language translator. Webcam frames stay in your browser; hand landmarks are sent to this server and may be saved for debugging. Uploaded videos are processed on the server.
